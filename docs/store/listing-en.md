- **Keywords (App Store, 100):** train,trains,platform,departures,national rail,live,station,uk,railway,times,delays

## Description

Open the app and see the next trains from the station you're standing at — with the platform, in big type.

• Finds your nearest National Rail station on its own: no searching, no setup.
• Live departures: on time, expected time, delayed or cancelled, with the reason.
• The platform as soon as it's announced.
• Tap a train to see the stops it still has to make, with expected times.
• Follow a train (Android): you get a notification if its platform changes, it runs late or it's cancelled, even with the app closed.
• Service alerts for the station, one tap away.
• Filter by destination to see only your trains.
• Pick any other station in Great Britain by name or code.

Free. No ads, no account, no tracking. Your location stays on your phone.

Sources
• Live train times, platforms and service alerts: National Rail (Darwin), https://www.nationalrail.co.uk/ — licensed through the Rail Data Marketplace, https://raildata.org.uk/
• Station locations: NaPTAN, Department for Transport, Open Government Licence, https://www.data.gov.uk/dataset/ff93ffc1-6656-47d8-9155-85ea0b8f2251/national-public-transport-access-nodes-naptan

What Platform is an independent app. It does not represent any government entity and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Department for Transport, National Rail or any train operator.
