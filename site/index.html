What Platform

The next trains and their platform at your nearest station in Great Britain.

Privacy policy

We don't collect any data. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your location

If you allow it, the app uses your location only on your phone to find the nearest station. Your location is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can say no and pick a station by hand.

What our server receives

To show departures, the app asks our server for the departures of one station, identified by its three-letter code (for example KGX). That is all the request contains. Our server keeps no logs of these requests and does not record your IP address.

What stays on your phone

The last station you looked at and your destination filters are stored on your phone so the app opens where you left it. They never leave the device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Data sources

Train times: National Rail (Darwin, via the Rail Data Marketplace). Station locations: NaPTAN, Department for Transport, Open Government Licence.
